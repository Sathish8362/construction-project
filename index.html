Everything You Need to Know Before Building

Plain answers to common questions regarding timelines, fixed pricing, construction materials, and municipal permits.

Typical timelines depend on scope: custom new homes usually take 8 to 14 months, major renovations and extensions take 4 to 7 months, and custom commercial fit-outs take 2 to 4 months.

Every contract includes a binding schedule with agreed milestone dates. We schedule subcontractor trades in parallel and order long-lead materials in advance to protect against delays.

Our fixed-price quote is 100% itemized. We specify every material brand, concrete specification, plumbing fixture, and labor line item before work begins.

Once the contract is signed, that price is locked. The only way the cost changes is if you explicitly request a written change order (for example, choosing a different countertop or altering room dimensions mid-project).

We only source structural lumber, high-strength certified concrete mixes (minimum 3,500 to 4,000 PSI), corrosion-resistant rebar, and premium waterproofing membranes from trusted suppliers.

For finishes—such as roofing tiles, windows, flooring, and electrical switches—we provide a curated catalog of high-durability options, or you can supply your own preferred architectural fixtures.

Yes, 100%. You do not need to stand in line at the city planning office. We manage the entire regulatory process: submission of architectural and structural blueprints, zoning clearance, soil test filings, and scheduling required municipal inspections (foundation, framing, plumbing, rough-in electrical, and final occupancy).

Never pay 100% upfront. Our payments are tied directly to verified construction milestones. A standard project is divided into 5 or 6 clear milestone disbursements (e.g., initial deposit, foundation poured, framing & roof locked, rough-in trades approved, interior finishes, and final punch-list handover).
